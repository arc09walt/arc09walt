# Songwright

**github.com/arc09walt**

*Software built by hand in Michigan — a foundry man's tools, guides, and music rooms.*

Everything here is free (MIT), built one piece at a time, and every consequential press stays the owner's — the guide prepares, points, checks, and receipts.

## The shelf

### [Servercrafter](https://github.com/arc09walt/Servercrafter) — NEW · v1.2

The Server Builder Guider — walks a first-timer through raising their own server in three acts: the house, the sidewalk, the street door. Published today.

### [DeerGetter](https://github.com/arc09walt/DeerGetter)

A Michigan roadkill-salvage board. Its porch server runs live today on its operator's own PC, reachable from the street through its tunnel door. App v1.1.

### [Music-Room](https://github.com/arc09walt/Music-Room)

The full-band music room. GitHub carries the showcase — Amazon Appstore placement is pending, its own page will carry the terms when it lands.

### [Beats](https://github.com/arc09walt/Beats)

The beat room. Public 1.22 — Front of house is free — special features available. The front of house — the drummer, the house kit, the Music Room core — is free, in full. The special features (the Toy Cabinet, the Workbench, MP3 export) live in the full Music Room app, a separate purchase in-app.

### [Deep-Diver](https://github.com/arc09walt/Deep-Diver)

An audit-not-collection research diver for mostly-declassified public archives. v1.2.

### [Muzzle](https://github.com/arc09walt/Muzzle)

Quiets Windows ad ID, telemetry, and tracker call-outs — with RESTORE to undo it all. v1.2.3.

### [TheGatekeeper](https://github.com/arc09walt/TheGatekeeper)

An OpenWrt router-flashing guide for the E8450. The guide never uploads firmware or touches a password — those presses are yours.

### [Rosetta-Driver](https://github.com/arc09walt/Rosetta-Driver)

Reads your driver list and translates it into plain words: fine, old, broken. And it doesn't stop at naming — if you choose it, it walks you through the fix: restore point first, three honest doors, a receipt at the end. It never installs or replaces anything itself; every press stays yours.

### [Onedrive-remover](https://github.com/arc09walt/Onedrive-remover)

Takes OneDrive off a Windows PC, cleanly.
